Questions

Do my vendors need an account?

No. They get a link, check their tasks, and they're done.

Can I change the default task times?

Yes. Every task length is editable, and your changes carry into future weddings.

What happens if the couple changes the dinner time?

Update it once and TaskTie shifts the timeline and asks the vendors it affects to re-confirm.

Will it help me keep up with vendors?

Yes. Log each email or call, see who's due for a check-in, and hit milestones at 8, 4, 2 and 1 weeks before the wedding.

Can I take some tasks myself?

Yes. "You (planner)" is an owner option for any task, like collecting cards and gifts.

# Ad Script

- **Format:** 30-second vertical video
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels (where wedding planners share their work)
- **Target audience:** Solo, independent wedding planners juggling 10–14 weddings
- **Goal / CTA:** Visit the TaskTie web page and start a first wedding free
- **Tone:** Relatable and a little funny behind the scenes, then calm and confident on the wedding day
- **Structure:** The first half shows the weeks *before* the wedding (the planner's desk, behind the scenes). The second half shows the wedding day, where that prep pays off.

## Script

| Time | Visual | Audio / Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **HOOK (behind the scenes):** Late night at the planner's desk. A wall calendar shows "3 weeks out," and a coffee mug sits next to a laptop. | "Three weeks before the wedding. Fourteen vendors. One question:" | 3 weeks out. 14 vendors. |
| 0:03–0:09 | **THE OLD WAY:** Quick cuts: an inbox stacked with 14 vendor threads, a messy spreadsheet, a sticky note reading "Water glasses?? Caterer or venue?" | "Who's doing what?" | Who's doing what? |
| 0:09–0:18 | **SETUP IN TASKTIE (behind the scenes):** The planner adds vendors, and the task list fills in. Vendors check off their tasks on their phones. A coral "No owner: move guests to dinner" badge appears, then an amber "Time conflict: photos vs. license signing" badge. The planner taps to fix both, and the day-of timeline builds itself. | "TaskTie sends every vendor one checklist, flags anything nobody claimed or two vendors did, and builds your timeline before the big day." | Every task, tied to a vendor |
| 0:18–0:27 | **WEDDING DAY (the payoff):** Quick, warm shots: the couple signs the marriage license with the officiant, then walks out for photos. The venue coordinator guides guests from cocktail hour to dinner. A server fills water glasses down a long table. The planner glances at the timeline on their phone and smiles. | "So on the wedding day, everyone already knows their part." | 5:45 License signed ✓ · 6:00 Photos ✓ · 6:15 Guests seated ✓ |
| 0:27–0:30 | **CTA:** TaskTie logo on ivory, sage button | "Start your first wedding free at TaskTie." | Start free → TaskTie |

## Music & sound
- **0:00–0:09:** Quiet and a little tense. Keyboard clicks and stacking email-notification pings.
- **0:09–0:18:** The music picks up, with a soft "check" sound each time a vendor claims a task.
- **0:18–0:30:** A light, upbeat acoustic track swells over wedding-day ambiance (glasses clinking, crowd murmur).

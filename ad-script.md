# Ad Script

- **Format:** 30-second square video, 1080×1080 at 60 fps (H.264 MP4 with music, no voiceover). File: `media/ad/tasktie-ad-30s.mp4`
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels (where wedding planners share their work). *Note:* Reels is a vertical 9:16 format, so this square video plays with empty bands above and below it.
- **Target audience:** Solo, independent wedding planners juggling 10–14 weddings
- **Goal / CTA:** Visit the TaskTie web page and start a first wedding free
- **Tone:** Relatable and a little funny behind the scenes, then calm and confident on the wedding day
- **Structure:** One continuous motion-graphic take with no cuts: each scene grows out of the one before it. The first half shows the weeks *before* the wedding (the planner's desk, the inbox, setting up TaskTie). The second half shows the wedding day, where that prep pays off. Where the app appears, an on-screen cursor drives it with real clicks.
- **Colors & fonts:** Inter only. Background #FAFCF3, text #2C3A28, grassy lime #8CC152 (logo), light lime #C8E6A0 (checks, caption labels), soft green #D7EDB8 (desk, chips), deep grass #2F4A24 and #4E7A2E ("Start free" button), berry pinks #E07A9B / #B23A62 / #FBE3EA ("No owner" flag), honeydew yellow #F8F2C4 ("Time conflict" chip, sticky note), honeydew #F4F8DC, light cherry pink #F4C6D3 (mug, dinner area).

## Music & sound
- **Track:** "Fun at the Farm" from Mixkit (free under the Mixkit license), about 80 BPM. Every scene change (0:03, 0:09, 0:18, 0:27) lands on a beat.
- **0:00–0:09:** The music is muffled and quiet. Keyboard clicks and stacking email pings, plus a paper swish and a sticky-note thump.
- **0:09–0:18:** The music opens up to full sound. Cursor clicks, a soft "check" each time a vendor claims a task, and a two-note alert when each flag appears.
- **0:18–0:30:** The music lifts a little more. Pen scratches, checks, a camera shutter, and glass clinks as the water glasses fill. A soft chime on the logo, and the music fades out over the last second.

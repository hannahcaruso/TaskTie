# Quick Survey (30 seconds)

**Send to:** Wedding planners who coordinate vendors
**Build in:** Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. All questions are multiple choice, so nobody has to type.

---

## Form title
Quick Question for Wedding Planners

## Form description
> 3 quick questions, about 30 seconds. Thank you!

---

**1. Has a wedding-day task ever been missed or doubled up because it wasn't clear which vendor was doing it?**
- Yes, often
- Yes, sometimes
- Rarely
- Never

*Required*

**2. About how long do you spend emailing one vendor to sort out who's doing what?**
- Less than 15 minutes
- 15–30 minutes
- 30–60 minutes
- More than 1 hour

*Required*

**3. Would a checklist that flags tasks no vendor has claimed be useful to you?**
- Yes
- Maybe
- No

*Required*

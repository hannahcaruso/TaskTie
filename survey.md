# Survey: Vendor Coordination for Wedding Planners

**Send to:** 1–2 wedding planners who coordinate vendors (not classmates)
**Build in:** Google Forms or Microsoft Forms
**Answers due:** Thursday, November 12

---

## Form title
Who's Doing What? A Quick Survey for Wedding Planners

## Form description
> I'm a student researching how wedding planners coordinate tasks with their vendors. This survey has 5 questions and takes about 3 minutes. Your name won't be shared. If possible, please respond by **Thursday, November 12**. Thank you!

---

## Questions

**1. Does this happen to you?**
At a wedding you planned, has a task ever been missed, done by two vendors, or scheduled at the same time as something else because it wasn't clear which vendor was handling it?
*(Examples: no one moved guests from cocktail hour to dinner, the caterer and venue staff both thought the other was filling water glasses, or the photographer planned photos while the couple needed to sign the marriage license.)*
- Yes, often
- Yes, sometimes
- Rarely
- Never

*Form type: Multiple choice (required)*

**2. How often?**
Of the weddings you planned in the last year, about how many had at least one of these mix-ups?
- None
- 1 or 2 weddings
- A few weddings
- About half
- Most or all

*Form type: Multiple choice (required)*

**3. What does it cost you in time?**
For one wedding, about how long do you spend emailing a single vendor to work out what they will and won't handle on the day?
- Less than 15 minutes
- 15–30 minutes
- 30–60 minutes
- More than 1 hour

*Form type: Multiple choice (required)*

**4. What does it cost you when it goes wrong?**
Think of a time a task was missed or doubled up. What happened on the wedding day, and what did it take to fix it?

*Form type: Paragraph / Long answer (optional)*

**5. How do you handle it today?**
How do you keep track of which vendor is doing which task? (Check all that apply.)
- Email threads
- Spreadsheet
- Planning software or app
- Checklist or questionnaire sent to vendors
- Paper checklist or binder
- Memory or experience
- Other: ________

*Form type: Checkboxes with "Other" option (required)*

---

## How each answer tests problem.md

| Question | What problem.md says | It disagrees if... |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Does it happen? | Tasks get missed, doubled up, or conflict | Answer is "Rarely" or "Never" |
| 2. How often? | It happens often enough to cause stress | Answer is "None" or "1 or 2 weddings" |
| 3. Time cost | About 45 minutes per vendor | Answer is "Less than 15 minutes" |
| 4. Day-of cost | Missed tasks cause stress and bad flow | Stories describe small, easy fixes |
| 5. Current method | Email, memory, sometimes a spreadsheet | They already use software that solves it |

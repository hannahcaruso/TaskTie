# Web Page (Landing Page Copy & Design Spec)

## Design system
| Token | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Off-white with a hint of green `#FAFCF3` | Page background |
| Surface | White `#FFFFFF` | Cards, timeline panel |
| Text | Dark green-charcoal `#2C3A28` | Headlines and body text |
| Muted text | `#5D6A57` | Subheadlines, captions |
| Primary | Deep grass `#4E7A2E` | Buttons, links |
| Primary hover | `#3E6324` | Button hover |
| Accent: lime | Grassy lime `#8CC152` / light lime `#C8E6A0` | Logo, card accents, final button |
| Accent: berry | Cherry pink `#F4C6D3` / pale berry pink `#FBE3EA` | Problem band, "Most popular" tag |
| Accent: honeydew | `#F4F8DC` | "How it works" and pricing bands |
| Dark band | Deep grass `#2F4A24` | Final call to action |
| Flag: unclaimed | Pale berry pink `#FBE3EA` with deep berry text `#B23A62` | "No owner" badge |
| Flag: double-claimed / conflict | Honeydew yellow `#F8F2C4` with `#7A6410` text | "Two owners" / "Time conflict" badge |
| Border | `#E2E9D4` | Card and divider lines |

- **Fonts:** Headlines in *Playfair Display* (600), body in *Inter* (400/500). Body text 18px, line height 1.6. Visitors can change the text size (A/A/A control in the header).
- **Buttons:** Deep grass background, white text, 12px corner radius, padding 14px × 28px.
- **Layout:** Max content width 1120px, centered. On phones, one column with 16px side margins.
- **Feel:** Calm, organized, wedding-elegant. Lots of white space and no stock photos of rings.

## 2. The problem (blush band)
**Heading:** Who's moving guests to dinner?

You've emailed 14 vendors. The caterer thinks the venue is filling water glasses, and the venue thinks the caterer is. The photographer booked the couple for photos at the exact time they're supposed to sign the marriage license. You won't find out until the wedding day.

## 3. The solution
**Heading:** One list. One owner per task. Zero guessing.

TaskTie replaces the email back-and-forth with a single checklist each vendor fills out. It catches every task no one claimed, or two vendors claimed, and builds your day-of timeline for you.

## 4. Features (3 white cards in a row; stacked on phones)
- **Never start from scratch:** A full standard task list for every vendor type, plus suggestions for the tasks unique to this wedding.
- **Gaps get flagged:** A coral "No owner" or amber "Two owners" badge appears the moment a task needs you.
- **Your timeline builds itself:** Each task gets a realistic default time, and when something changes, everything updates.

## 5. How it works (numbered steps, sage circles)
1. **Add your vendors:** TaskTie loads the right tasks for each one.
2. **Send checklists:** Each vendor checks off what they'll handle. No email threads.
3. **Fix the flags, get your timeline:** Assign what's left and print your day-of run sheet.

## 6. Why it matters (stat row, 3 columns)
- **13:** average vendors per wedding ([The Knot / WeddingPro](https://pros.weddingpro.com/blog/entrepreneurship/real-wedding-study-vendor-insights/))

## 8. FAQ
**Do my vendors need an account?**
No. They get a link, check their tasks, and they're done.

**Can I change the default task times?**
Yes. Every task length is editable, and your changes carry into future weddings.

**What happens if the couple changes the dinner time?**
Update it once and TaskTie shifts the timeline and alerts the vendors it affects.

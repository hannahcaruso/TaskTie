## Catering

**Setup**

- [ ] Set up and dress guest tables and chairs
- [ ] Lay linens, overlays and napkins (folded or placed per design)
- [ ] Set place settings: flatware, plates, chargers, glassware
- [ ] Place menu cards, place cards and table numbers (confirm if florist or planner does this instead)
- [ ] Fill water glasses before guests are seated
- [ ] Set up buffet or station tables, chafers and signage
- [ ] Set up cocktail hour tables and high-tops

**Service**

- [ ] Pass hors d'oeuvres during cocktail hour
- [ ] Prepare and deliver plates for couple and wedding party
- [ ] Serve dinner by the agreed timeline (plated, buffet or family style)
- [ ] Handle dietary and allergy meals by table and seat
- [ ] Refill water and clear courses
- [ ] Pour champagne for toasts
- [ ] Feed vendors (photographer, DJ, planner) at the agreed time
- [ ] Cut and plate the wedding cake after the cake cutting
- [ ] Serve coffee, dessert and late-night snacks

**Wrap-up**

- [ ] Box a to-go meal and top tier of cake for the couple
- [ ] Return cake knife, toasting flutes and keepsakes to the family
- [ ] Clear tables, strip linens and pack rentals for pickup
- [ ] Remove trash and leave kitchen per venue rules

## DJ / band

**Ceremony**

- [ ] Set up ceremony sound system and microphones
- [ ] Mic the officiant (lavalier) and set a mic for readers
- [ ] Play prelude music as guests arrive
- [ ] Cue processional, couple's entrance, recessional on the planner's signal

**Reception announcements**

- [ ] Announce the start of cocktail hour and where to go
- [ ] Ask guests to take their seats
- [ ] Announce the wedding party and couple's grand entrance
- [ ] Announce dinner and release tables to the buffet, if applicable
- [ ] Introduce toasts and hand off the mic
- [ ] Announce first dance, parent dances and cake cutting
- [ ] Announce bouquet and garter toss, if included
- [ ] Announce last dance and the send-off

**Music and logistics**

- [ ] Play cocktail hour and dinner background music
- [ ] Follow the couple's must-play and do-not-play lists
- [ ] Coordinate timing cues with planner, caterer and photographer
- [ ] Provide dance floor lighting or uplighting, if contracted
- [ ] Strike equipment by the venue's load-out time

## Photographer and videographer

- [ ] Capture getting-ready and detail shots (rings, invitation, dress, shoes)
- [ ] Run the first look, if planned
- [ ] Cover the ceremony without blocking guest views
- [ ] Lead family and wedding party formals from the shot list
- [ ] Photograph the room and tables before guests enter
- [ ] Cover entrances, toasts, first dance, cake cutting and send-off
- [ ] Take sunset or night portraits at the agreed time
- [ ] Capture ceremony and toast audio (videographer)

## Florist

- [ ] Deliver bouquets, boutonnieres and corsages to getting-ready locations
- [ ] Pin boutonnieres and corsages, if contracted
- [ ] Install ceremony arch, aisle and altar florals
- [ ] Set centerpieces and head table florals
- [ ] Decorate the cake with fresh flowers, if requested
- [ ] Move ceremony florals to the reception (flip), if contracted
- [ ] Pick up vessels and installations after the event

## Venue coordinator

- [ ] Unlock spaces and confirm vendor load-in times and entrances
- [ ] Set the ceremony chairs and reception floor plan
- [ ] Manage climate, lighting and restrooms
- [ ] Enforce venue rules (candles, confetti, sparklers, noise curfew)
- [ ] Execute the rain plan, if needed
- [ ] Lock up and confirm all vendors have left

## Bakery

- [ ] Deliver and assemble the cake at the agreed time
- [ ] Set up the cake table and dessert display
- [ ] Place the cake topper
- [ ] Pick up cake stand and display pieces, if rented

## Bar service

- [ ] Set up the bar, ice, garnishes and glassware
- [ ] Serve the signature cocktails
- [ ] Check IDs and refuse service to intoxicated guests
- [ ] Pour the champagne toast, if not handled by catering
- [ ] Close the bar at the agreed time and announce last call

## Rentals

- [ ] Deliver tables, chairs, linens, tent, dance floor or lounge furniture
- [ ] Set up and break down items, if contracted (otherwise confirm who does)
- [ ] Pick up all items at the agreed time

## Officiant

- [ ] Lead the ceremony rehearsal, if contracted
- [ ] Arrive early and meet the couple and planner
- [ ] Perform the ceremony
- [ ] Sign the marriage license with the couple and witnesses
- [ ] File the license, or confirm who will

## Hair and makeup

- [ ] Follow the getting-ready schedule by person
- [ ] Finish the couple and wedding party before photo time
- [ ] Leave a touch-up kit, or stay for touch-ups if contracted

## Transportation

- [ ] Pick up the couple and wedding party on schedule
- [ ] Run guest shuttles between hotel, ceremony and reception
- [ ] Provide the getaway car at send-off
- [ ] Confirm the driver's day-of number and backup plan

## Gaps to assign

These tasks are often assumed to be someone else's job. Write a name next to each one before the wedding day.

- [ ] Set out guest book, card box, favors and welcome sign
- [ ] Place escort cards and the seating chart
- [ ] Light candles, and confirm the venue allows open flame
- [ ] Hand out programs and reserve front-row seats
- [ ] Cue the wedding party to line up for the processional and entrances
- [ ] Collect the cards and gifts and give them to a named family member
- [ ] Hand vendor tips and final payments to each vendor
- [ ] Pack the couple's personal items, decor and leftover alcohol at the end
- [ ] Supply sparklers or send-off items, and a lighter
